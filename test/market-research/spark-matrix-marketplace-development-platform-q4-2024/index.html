Frequently asked questions

What is a marketplace development platform?

A marketplace development platform is software for creating, managing and scaling multi-vendor online marketplaces. Typical capabilities include multi-vendor support, catalogue and order management, payments and commissions, and partner onboarding. This report explains how these platforms are evolving and how vendors compare.

Which vendors are evaluated in the SPARK Matrix™: Marketplace Development Platform, Q4 2024?

The report evaluates 14 vendors: AppDirect, Arrow Electronics, Arcadier, CloudBlue, IZBERG, Marketplacer, Mirakl, Oro Inc., Persistent Systems, Spryker, Sitecore, Tradeshift, Vendasta and VTEX. The full vendor profiles and positioning are included in the paid report.
