What is SPARK Matrix™?

SPARK Matrix™ (Strategic Performance Assessment and Ranking) is QKS Group’s proprietary evaluation framework designed to analyze and rank technology vendors based on two critical dimensions:

  • Technology Excellence
  • Customer Impact

It provides a visual and strategic comparison of leading solution providers, making it easier for businesses to identify the right technology partners aligned with their goals.

How Does SPARK Matrix™ Stand Out?

A Framework Built for Clarity and Credibility.

SPARK Matrix™ is more than a quadrant, it’s a multi-dimensional market positioning tool built to reflect the realities of modern B2B tech evaluation. Here’s what makes it unique:

3x2 Strategic Matrix Design

Unlike traditional 2x2 grids, SPARK Matrix™ uses a 3x2 quadrant structure, categorizing vendors into:

  • Leaders & Emerging Leaders – High Technology Excellence & High Customer Impact
  • Contenders & Strong Contenders – Strong potential with growing customer traction
  • Aspirants – Emerging players still developing key capabilities

This layered structure enables a more nuanced and realistic vendor assessment.

Unique Recognitions

We go beyond the matrix with analyst-recognized categories:

  • Emerging Innovator – Recognizes vendors bringing disruptive innovation despite limited current adoption.
  • Ace Performer – Highlights vendors delivering exceptional customer satisfaction or performance in specific regional or industry segments.
